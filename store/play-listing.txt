Paint the Town: Date Nights

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
Plan date nights you'll actually go on — ideas, plans, and calendar in one app.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
-----------------------------------------------------
"We should go out more." You've both said it. Paint the Town is how it actually happens.

A date-night planner for couples: collect ideas when inspiration strikes, turn them into concrete plans, put them on the calendar, and keep a running record of the nights that were worth repeating.

IDEAS WORTH KEEPING
Save date ideas the moment you think of them — that restaurant someone mentioned, the drive-in, the ramen place from the show. Browse them when the "what do you want to do?" conversation stalls.

FROM IDEA TO PLAN
Pick the idea, pick the night, and Paint the Town puts it on your calendar with reminders — so Thursday-you actually follows through on Sunday-you's good intentions.

REMEMBER THE GOOD ONES
Rate and note the nights you loved. Build your own greatest-hits list for anniversaries, birthdays, and "we need a win" weeks.

MADE FOR TWO
Simple enough that both of you will actually use it. Sign in on both phones and share the plan.

Date night shouldn't be a project. Open the app, pick a night, paint the town.
