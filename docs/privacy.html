Privacy policy & support

DRAFT — REWRITE BEFORE PUBLISHING: Paint the Town uses accounts and a Supabase backend; this local-first template does NOT match the app. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers of ours. The only data that ever leaves your phone is what you send yourself — invoices and payment reminders through apps you choose, backups you export, and purchases processed by the app store.

What Paint the Town stores

Your date ideas, plans, notes, and settings are stored in a local database on your device. We cannot see, access, or recover this data — it never reaches us. Deleting the app deletes the data, so export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Calendar access

If you add a date night to your calendar, Paint the Town uses the calendar permission to create that event on the calendar you choose — that is the only thing it is used for. The app never reads your other events for any purpose beyond showing your own plans.

Purchases

Paint the Town Pro is an auto-renewing subscription purchased through the app store on your device (Apple App Store or Google Play). Purchases are processed by the store and validated by RevenueCat, our purchase-management provider, using an anonymous identifier and your store receipt. This does not include your name, email address, students, or any content from the app.

Backups

Backups are plain files you export and keep wherever you like. They are yours; nothing is uploaded anywhere by the app.
